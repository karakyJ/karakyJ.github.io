Privacy Policy

Text on Photos (كتابة على الصور)  ·  Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Text on Photos ("the App"), developed by JimLizApp, handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Text on Photos does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not upload your photos or edited images to any server.
  • All photo editing happens entirely on your device.
  • Edited images are saved directly to your device's gallery.

2. Photos and Camera Access

The App requests access to your device's camera and photo gallery solely to allow you to select or capture a photo for editing. Photos are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any external server.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain device information and use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage data for ad targeting

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy .

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases processed through the Google Play Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. All transactions are handled securely by Google. For details, see Google Play Terms of Service .

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases (Terms)

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

7. Data Security

Since all processing happens on-device and we do not collect personal data, there is no data stored on our servers that could be at risk. Your photos and edits remain entirely on your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
