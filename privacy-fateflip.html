FATE FLIP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Welcome to Fate Flip, a coin flip decision-making app developed by JimLizApp. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. About Fate Flip

Fate Flip is an entertainment app that helps users make decisions through animated coin flips. It includes various flip modes, coin skins, a best-of game mode, and a custom coin creator. The app is designed for personal entertainment and is suitable for all ages.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data.

All user data — including your preferences, unlocked content, custom coins, flip history, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

3. Custom Coin Images

If you use the Custom Coin Creator feature and add photos from your device gallery, those images are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded, shared, or accessible outside of the Fate Flip app.

4. Advertising

Fate Flip uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to free users. AdMob may collect certain information to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • General location (country/region level)
  • App usage data for ad targeting

Users who purchase the Fate Flip Premium upgrade will not see any advertisements. For more information on how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

5. In-App Purchases

Fate Flip offers optional in-app purchases (Custom Coin Creator for $0.99 and Fate Flip Premium for $4.99). All purchases are processed entirely through Google Play. We do not collect, store, or have access to any payment information. Purchase records are managed by Google Play.

6. Permissions

The app may request the following device permissions:

  • Internet — required for loading advertisements
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback during coin flips
  • Read Media Images — only when you choose to add a photo to a custom coin
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob for ad personalization
  • Billing — required for in-app purchases via Google Play

No microphone, camera, location, contacts, or any other sensitive permissions are used.

7. Children's Privacy

Fate Flip is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains advertising which complies with applicable regulations including COPPA and Google Play Families Policy.

8. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases

9. Data Security

Since all user data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security settings. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with a PIN, password, or biometric lock.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
